# The Upper Ledger

*Where the old money lives and the new money visits.*

---

## Overview

The Upper Ledger is [[Offledger]]'s wealthiest district, a neighborhood that sits slightly above the rest of the plane in both altitude and attitude. The streets are paved with compressed contract paper, centuries of fulfilled obligations underfoot. The air smells like expensive parchment and old coins.

The district angles away from the rest of [[Offledger]] like a jaw set in disapproval. Its buildings are older, darker, and quieter than the commercial chaos of the lower districts. No signage. No advertisements. No indication of what happens inside these walls, because everyone who matters already knows.

---

## Characteristics

- **Architecture:** Dark wood and brass, heavy ironwork, buildings that predate Veezy's arrival in [[Offledger]] by decades. Everything is maintained to a standard that suggests the owners have never needed to think about maintenance.
- **Streets:** Compressed contract paper underfoot. Walking through the Upper Ledger means treading on centuries of fulfilled obligations. The ground crunches faintly, like dry leaves made of money.
- **Atmosphere:** Quiet. Measured. The district has its own social gravity; voices lower naturally, gestures shrink, posture improves. People here don't shout. They don't need to.
- **Security:** No visible guards. The doors remember who is invited. Unauthorized visitors find that streets rearrange subtly, leading them back to the district boundary without drama. Persistent intruders attract the attention of private debt-wardens, who are polite, efficient, and empowered to collect.

---

## Notable Residents

- **[[Aldenmere Voss]]** (deceased) — Former custodian of [[The Instrument of Contested Debt]]. His estate occupies a corner lot that angles away from the street like a man turning his back on a conversation.
- **[[Tesslyn Voss]]** — Aldenmere's niece. Maintains a room at the estate.
- **[[Councilor Dresh]]** — Member of [[Offledger]]'s Debt Arbitration Council. Keeps offices in the district.

---

## Narrative Function

The Upper Ledger exists to make the PCs feel underdressed, underfunded, and underestimated. It is the neighborhood where the people who own the systems live, as opposed to the people who work within them. When the PCs arrive for [[11 - The Debt Collector's Wake|The Recession]], the Upper Ledger should feel like enemy territory, not because anyone is hostile, but because the PCs are being measured by standards they didn't agree to.
